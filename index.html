[ SYSTEM GUIDE ]

⚔️ LEVELING SYSTEM

The System monitors your progress from Level 1 to 90. XP requirements increase polynomially across tiers.

  • • Each level up grants 5 Attribute Points.
  • • Attributes (STR, INT, AGI, VIT, SNS) are capped at 999.
  • • Points allocated beyond the cap are stored in the Overflow Pool.

🏆 RANKING SYSTEM

Your Player Rank is determined by your current level:

E-Rank: Lvl 1-10
D-Rank: Lvl 11-20
C-Rank: Lvl 21-35
B-Rank: Lvl 36-50
A-Rank: Lvl 51-70
S-Rank: Lvl 71-90

⚠️ PENALTY SYSTEM

Failure to complete missions results in severe system penalties.

  • • Normal Quests: 5% XP penalty per missed quest.
  • • Mandatory Quests: 10% XP penalty per missed quest.
  • • Total daily penalty is capped at 25% of current XP.
  • • VIT Attribute: Reduces XP loss by up to 50%.
  • • CRITICAL STATE: 3 consecutive days of missing mandatory quests halves all future XP gains until recovery.
